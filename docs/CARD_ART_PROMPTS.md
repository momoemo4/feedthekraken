# Card artwork prompt pack

These prompts generate illustration layers only. Direction color, card title, rules text, quantity marker, and accessibility labels should be rendered by the UI so all copies remain readable and consistent.

## Shared art direction

Append this block to every card prompt:

> Portrait 2:3 collectible board-game card illustration, hand-painted late-1990s dark nautical fantasy strategy-game aesthetic, realistic maritime materials, dramatic chiaroscuro, restrained teal, storm-grey, tarnished brass and aged parchment palette, painterly brushwork with crisp focal silhouette, atmospheric depth, premium tabletop-game finish. Full-bleed illustration with a quiet lower area for UI rules text. No typography, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no watermark, no modern objects, no cartoon style, no glossy mobile-game rendering, no copied characters or existing game symbols.

## Navigation cards

Direction frames are applied by the UI: Yellow/North uses weathered ochre-gold, Blue/East uses cold navy/cyan, and Red/West uses muted crimson/rust. Do not ask the image model to draw navigation arrows or card text.

### Cult Uprising — 6 Yellow cards

> On the moonlit deck of an old sailing ship, hooded sailors perform a secret ritual around a wet wooden kraken idol; faint golden candlelight, black tentacles barely visible beneath the waves, ominous but not gory, centered ritual circle and strong top-down maritime composition.

### Drunk — 4 Blue and 5 Red cards

> A disgraced ship captain slumped beside a rolling rum barrel on a storm-tossed deck, captain's hat slipping away in the wind, crew silhouettes watching from the rigging, unstable lantern light and dramatic waves, readable single-character focal point.

The same base illustration can serve both colors; the UI direction frame differentiates Blue and Red copies.

### Disarmed — 2 Blue cards

> A navigator's flintlock pistol and powder horn knocked from their hands by a crashing wave, weapons sliding across rain-soaked deck planks toward the sea, cold blue storm light, dynamic diagonal composition, no injury.

### Mermaid — 2 Red cards

> A mysterious sea-worn mermaid surfacing beside a lantern-lit ship at night, holding three waterlogged navigation cards just above the waves, alluring but unsettling expression, crimson sunset fading into deep teal water, realistic anatomy and maritime folklore atmosphere.

### Telescope — 2 Red cards

> A sailor at the bow studies a single distant omen through an antique brass spyglass, one hidden navigation card reflected in the telescope lens, red storm clouds behind distant reefs, strong brass focal detail, secret knowledge mood.

### Armed — 2 Red cards

> A determined navigator receiving a loaded flintlock pistol from an open shipboard weapons chest, powder smoke and warm red lantern light, wet ropes and dark ocean behind, heroic but grounded maritime realism, no firing and no gore.

## Cult Ritual cards

Ritual cards share an aged black-violet frame with tarnished gold filigree and a subtle kraken motif added by the UI. Each resolved card remains visible face-up in the public ritual history.

### Conversion to Cult — 3 cards

> Inside a cramped candlelit ship cabin, a lone sailor accepts a small kraken medallion from hooded hands while their old naval colors lie folded in shadow, secret initiation, black seawater seeping between floorboards, violet-gold ritual light, identity deliberately ambiguous.

### Cult's Guns Stash — 1 card

> A hidden compartment beneath warped cabin floorboards packed with three ornate flintlock pistols, powder flasks and cult-marked cloth, opened by a hooded hand, warm candlelight against cold damp wood, exactly three prominent pistols.

### Cult Cabin Search — 1 card

> A hooded Cult Leader secretly examining three miniature crew portraits pinned inside a captain's logbook—Captain, Lieutenant and Navigator represented only by distinct naval silhouettes—dim cabin, occult magnifying lens, violet candle flame, atmosphere of private investigation.
